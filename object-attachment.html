How structures bind to objects

These two charts are the clinical heart of the archive: not only “what kind of subject,” but “what do they hold onto, at which step, and why.” Attachment is a logic of anxiety, not a shopping list of hobbies.

Why objects, in the chart’s own words

  • To reduce anxiety
  • To cover lack
  • To keep desire alive
  • To prove oneself
  • To hold control
  • To go on existing

The gold box: the object is a little solution knotted around lack. Some people remain in the symptom; some turn it into a sinthome. That fork is the clinic.
