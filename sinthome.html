Fourth ring · Seminar XXIII

Sinthome

Lacan respells symptôme as sinthome to mark a shift: not a coded message to interpret away, but a singular know-how that knots a life. On these charts it is the sixth step and the aim of every “ethical” row.

From symptom to sinthome

A symptom binds jouissance and tells a truth the subject does not want to know. Analysis can read it. A sinthome is what remains — and what can be used — when the subject stops fighting that kernel and makes a practice of it. Writing, a craft, a way of loving, a discipline: the form is idiosyncratic. That is the point.

Symptom

Repeats. Hurts. Protects. Often hidden from the subject’s official story. Aimed at covering lack or keeping the Other in play.

Sinthome

Still a repetition, but chosen as a style. Holds RSI together. Makes lack livable. Can be shared without being a demand.

The goal printed on every cycle chart

Almost every image in the July 29 set ends with the same box: knot the three registers (Symbolic–Imaginary–Real); make peace with lack; live a creative and singular life. That is the pedagogical definition of sinthome in this archive.

Failure of the knot

The charts are honest about the other side: if the sinthome is not fed, it becomes a tired duty; the subject may isolate, lose joy, or fall back into the old loop. A knot is a practice, not a trophy.

Joyce is the canonical literary case (a writing that held him). The charts translate that into ordinary clinic: each structure has a possible sinthome object — creation, a work, a body of thought — instead of only a control, gaze, fetish, or stabilizing object. Continue with object attachment.
