The formula

Step five on the cycle is the moment a symptom becomes an algorithm. The subject no longer only suffers a reply to trauma; they live by it. The charts draw this as a function: a repeated way of getting a bit of jouissance and a bit of safety.

Examples in the idiom of each structure

Obsessive

“If I control, delay, and do it right, I will be safe.” The formula is a fortress. Ethical rewrite: a plan that can fail and still be mine.

Hysteric

“If I keep you wanting, I exist.” The formula is a question that must not be answered. Ethical rewrite: a form of address that can also stand alone.

Perverse

“If I run the scene, lack will not show.” The formula is a script. Ethical rewrite: making and playing without turning people into props.

Psychotic

A private system that holds the world. Ethical rewrite: a practice (sinthome) that holds without sealing the subject off.

When the formula freezes

The “unresolved” cells say: flexibility dies; life becomes mechanical; new ties are hard; the subject cannot play. Therapy in this idiom is not “delete the formula.” It is to make it seasonal, shareable, and loyal to a desire rather than to a panic.

The next step after a living formula is a sinthome.
