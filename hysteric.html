The hysteric subject

The hysteric subject is organized around a question to the Other: What do you want? What am I for you? Desire is kept alive by not closing the answer. The Real appears as the opacity of the Other’s desire.

The hysteric’s engine

Lacan’s hysteric is not “theatrical personality.” It is a structure in which the subject’s being is referred to the Other’s desire. Meaning is produced by questioning. Proof is being the special object. Trauma is being dropped, replaced, unseen. The symptom keeps the Other’s desire in play — dissatisfaction, conversion, the unending riddle.
